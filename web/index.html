Recouper extracts at-risk money from failed payments, abandoned checkouts and overdue invoices, classifies each case by why it failed, proposes a bounded plan, and refuses anything the policy engine does not allow. A fifth of every batch is held back as an untouched control arm so the recovery number can be checked rather than believed.

  1. Start a run. Cases are extracted, classified and planned. Nothing is executed and no customer is contacted — every plan waits for you.
  2. Review. Open any case to see its classification, its proposed plan, and every policy check with the reason it passed or blocked.
  3. Approve. Approve one case or the whole batch. Policy re-runs immediately before each action, so approval can never push a denied action through.
  4. Verify. Every decision — yours and the system's — is a hash-chained audit record you can verify from this page.

Try Start with faults to watch the circuit breaker halt a batch mid-flight, then resume it from its checkpoint without repeating completed work.
